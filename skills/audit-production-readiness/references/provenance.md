## Interpretation boundaries

The handbook's BLOCKER/RISK/RECOMMENDED decisions are proposed local release policy derived from cited principles. They are not automatically a law or the cited standard's own universal release gate. Keep requirement separate from severity. Keep cited normative standards separate from informative notes, draft guidance, and local policy.

WCAG conformance needs the complete applicable criterion ledger and declared scope; grouped controls or a scanner score alone cannot establish it. Non-web mappings alone do not establish WCAG conformance. Signed artifacts alone do not prove correct source/builder or business-state behavior. Synthetic desk tests in the source illustrate gate semantics and false-green counterexamples; they are not product performance, security, device, or recovery evidence.

For a real release, check current official source status when adoption depends on changing platform rules, law, draft/final status, standard revisions, or unversioned documentation. Record what was checked and when. Do not silently replace the stored snapshot, call a draft final, or claim this translation is an accredited audit, certification, complete risk inventory, or zero-vulnerability guarantee.
