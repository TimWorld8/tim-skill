## Decision Summary

State GO or NO-GO, demonstrated failures, required evidence gaps, critical vetoes, and any accepted residual risk. Limit the decision to this exact candidate and context. Distinguish the assessment recommendation from human release approval and deployment.

## Coverage and Applicability

List every module with activation/exclusion rationale, reviewer, date, reopening trigger, and control counts. Show PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN / N/A / WAIVED by module and requirement. Keep RECOMMENDED backlog and original waived outcomes visible. Do not average away vetoes.

## Findings and Next Steps

For each finding record ID, control ID, requirement/severity, affected users/data/journey, expected/actual result, reproducer, evidence, impact/exposure, owner, remedy, target date, and retest criterion. Separate code-inspected concerns, observed test failures, simulations, and unverified claims.

## Evidence Index and Test Results

Link reviewed raw evidence to the release manifest. Record environment, tools/versions, commands, time, test inputs, measured thresholds, pass/fail outcomes, scenario coverage, limitations, and superseded results. Flag inaccessible, stale, or missing evidence.

## Accepted Risk

List each non-Critical RISK waiver, original FAIL/UNKNOWN, exact release/scope, authorized risk owner, reviewer, tested mitigation, expiry convention/timezone, remediation date, revocation and stop/rollback triggers. Never treat a waiver as PASS.

## Handover

Record support/escalation contacts, runbooks, operating constraints, post-release owner, approved audience/data/platform boundaries, monitoring, known gaps, and changes requiring reassessment. Obtain the recipient's receipt where required by the control.

## Signoff and Limitations

Record the designated human release decision and any conditions separately. State that this evidence-based review is not legal certification, a comprehensive guarantee, or a claim of zero vulnerabilities. Record source snapshot/version and freshness checks where standards or platform rules were adopted.
